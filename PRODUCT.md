## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework or build step. Deploy target: GitHub Pages (current portfolio lives at cuponk.github.io/portfolio).

## Users

Primary: recruiters and engineers screening candidates for AI/ML internships (Summer 2027 is the main target; availability from August 2026). They arrive from a résumé, LinkedIn, or an application link, usually on a laptop, and spend a minute or two deciding whether Justin is worth an interview. Secondary: Justin himself, as a record of what he has built.

## Product Purpose

Justin Aitken's personal portfolio. It must convince an ML hiring screen that Justin is a credible ML intern candidate: a practitioner who has already shipped full-stack software and is now building ML systems on top of a real CS and math foundation. Success: the visitor understands his path in seconds, opens a project, and reaches GitHub, LinkedIn, or email.

## Positioning

Practitioner-first: App Academy bootcamp (finished Sept 2023) → shipped full-stack apps → returned to community college (College of the Canyons) for the math → transferring to UC Santa Cruz, Fall 2026, CS B.A. with an Applied Math minor. Most ML-intern candidates have theory without shipped products, or shipped products without the math; Justin's story is both, in that order.

## Operating Context

Visitors come from job applications, LinkedIn, and GitHub. Read closely on a laptop; checked quickly on a phone.

## Evidence on Hand

Real: project names, stacks, scopes, Comparator live URL, education timeline, internships. Absent and must not be fabricated: model accuracy/error metrics for Snapcount, user counts, GPA, awards, testimonials. Any demonstration data shown (e.g. an example projection) must be labeled illustrative.

## Product Principles

1. Show the path, not a list: the bootcamp → math → UCSC → ML sequence is the argument.
2. Honest status over polish: in-progress work is labeled in progress; no invented numbers.
3. Every project says what it does, how it's built, and where to see it.
4. A screener should reach contact links within one scroll from anywhere.
